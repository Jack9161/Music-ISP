Zài Nà Yáo Yuǎn De Dì Fāng (Xinjiang Province)

This song comes from the province of Xinjiang. This is the largest province in the northwestern part of China. As this is such a vast province, the music varies from region to region. This is a common folk song of the Cossack people from Xinjiang. It can be sung by anyone but is highly suited for small groups or solos. Lyrical nature of the song makes learning the four verses very easy.

Technical Musical Analysis

Melody & Pitch: Built on a five-tone anhemitonic pentatonic scale framework (Yu mode), omitting Western semitones. The lyrical horizontal line utilizes expressive vocal arches and fluid portamento pitch bends to mirror the distinct linguistic tones of the regional text.
Texture & Space: Bypasses functional chord progressions in favor of a sparse, transparent heterophonic texture. Multiple traditional instruments or voices track the core nomadic melody simultaneously, inserting individualized ornamentation, delicate slides, and calculated structural silences.
Timbre & Tone: Maintains a steady, reflective duple meter (2/4 or 4/4) executed at an unhurried, narrative andante pace. The rhythmic framework features swaying, syncopated long-short note patterns that subtly mimic the natural speech cadence and movement of the western grasslands.

Pinyin Version

Zài nà yáo yuǎn de dì fāng

yǒu wèi hǎo gū niang,

rén men zǒu guò tā de zhàng fáng

dōu yào huí tóu liú lián de zhāng wàng.

Tā nà fěn hóng de xiǎo liǎn

hǎo xiàng hóng tài yáng,

tā nà huó pō dòng rén de yǎn jing

hǎo xiàng wǎn shàng míng mèi de yuè liang.

Wǒ yuàn pāo qì le cái chǎn

gēn tā qù fàng yáng,

měi tiān kàn she nà fěn hóng de xiǎo liǎn

hé nà měi lì jīng biān de yī shāng.

Wǒ yuàn zuò yī zhī xiǎo yáng

gēn zài tā shēn páng,

wǒ yuàn tā ná zhe xī xī de pí biān

bù duàn qīng qīng dǎ zài wǒ shēn shàng.

English Translation

In a far away place there is a good woman.

When people walk by her tent they turn their heads to look at her.

Her rosy cheeks are like the red sun.

Her bright eyes are like the bright moon at night.

I'm willing to give up all I have to go and raise sheep with her.

Everyday I look at her rosy face and her beautiful clothes that are lined with gold.

I wish to be like a little lamb following beside her.

I wish she would use her tool to guide me just as she does the little lamb.
