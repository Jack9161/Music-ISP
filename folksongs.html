Regional Folk Songs

Diverse Regional Traditions

China is comprised of many provinces. The unique music that comes from each of these regions is recognized throughout China. Whether it is the language, the story, or instruments used, each area has a rich and varied musical culture.

Mò LÌ Huā (Jasmine Flower)

Jiangnan Region

Originally dating from the Qing Dynasty, Mò Lì Huā is a xiǎodiào (short tune) known for its delicate and elegant melody. It describes a custom of giving jasmine flowers.

KĀNG DÌNG QÍNG GĒ (Kangding Love Song)

Sichuan Province

This popular love song from the mountainous Kangding area features clear Tibetan musical influences. It is sung with expressive freedom and soulful longing.

Wa Ha Ha

Xinjiang Province

A joyful, upbeat children's folk dance song from the Uyghur cultural tradition. It is widely taught in schools to celebrate happiness and seasonal beauty.

Zài Nà Yáo Yuǎn De Dì Fāng (In That Faraway Place)

Qinghai Province

Composed by Wang Luobin while collecting local regional folk tunes near northwestern Qinghai Lake. It blends traditional Han structures with ethnic minority styles.

Yǐ Méng Shān Xiǎo Diào

Shandong Province

A bright, lyrical folk song that celebrates the natural grandeur and loftiness of the dynamic mountain ranges along eastern China's coastlines.
