Kāng Dìng (Sichuan Province)

By Li Yi-Ruo

This popular Chinese folk song originates from the province of Sichuan. It was written in the 1930s by Li Yi-Ruo. He based the lyrics for the song on an event from his life. The song is sung fairly slowly with longing and meaning. There are many YouTube versions of this song. As the words repeat and the melody is simple, this song is easy to learn. This song can be sung throughout the year and can be sung by all ages.

Technical Musical Analysis

Melody & Pitch: Built entirely on a five-tone pentatonic scale framework, omitting standard Western semitones. The horizontal line relies on fluid pitch bends and portamento to track linguistic tones.
Texture & Space: Bypasses chord progressions in favor of a sparse, transparent heterophonic texture. Voices and instruments mirror the same melody with subtle variations, prioritizing structural silence.
Timbre & Tone: Emphasizes bright, acoustic instrument color profiles rooted in natural elements like wood, bamboo, and silk, backed by a forward nasal vocal production with minimal vibrato.

Pinyin Version

Pǎo mǎ liū liū de shān shàng,

yī duǒ liū liū de yún yao.

Duān duān liū liū de zhào zài,

kāng dìng liū liū de chéng yao.

Yuè liàng wān wān,

kāng dìng liū liū de chéng yao.

English Translation

High upon the mountain side

Floats a cloud so white

There lies the peaceful Kang Ding town

Bathed in silver moonlight

Moonlight shines so bright

Over Kang Ding town.

Chinese Text

跑马溜溜的山上一朵溜溜的云哟

端端溜溜的照在康定溜溜的城哟
