Wá hā hā (Xinjiang Province)

This song comes from the province of Xinjiang. This is the largest province in the northwestern part of China. As this is such a vast province, the music varies from region to region. This particular song is from the Uyghur people. This is a joyful song focusing on the beauty of the land and the importance of family.

Technical Musical Analysis

Melody & Pitch: Built entirely on a five-tone pentatonic scale framework, omitting standard Western semitones. The horizontal line relies on fluid pitch bends and portamento to track linguistic tones.
Texture & Space: Bypasses chord progressions in favor of a sparse, transparent heterophonic texture. Voices and instruments mirror the same melody with subtle variations, prioritizing structural silence.
Timbre & Tone: Emphasizes bright, acoustic instrument color profiles rooted in natural elements like wood, bamboo, and silk, backed by a forward nasal vocal production with minimal vibrato.

Pinyin Version

Wǒ mén de zǔ guó shì huā yuán,

huā yuán lǐ huā duǒ zhēn xiān yàn,

hé nuǎn de yáng guāng zhào yào zhe wǒ men,

měi gè rén liǎn shàng dōu xiào kāi yán.

Wá hā hā! Wá hā hā!

Měi gè rén liǎn shàng dōu xiào kāi yán.


Dà jiě jie nǐ ya gǎn kuài lái,

xiǎo dì di nǐ yě mò duǒ kāi,

shǒu lā zhe shǒu ya chàng qǐ nà gē ér,

wǒ mén de shēng huó duō yú kuài.

Wá hā hā! Wá hā hā!

Wǒ mén de shēng huó duō yú kuài.

English Translation

Our country is a garden.

In the garden, the brightly colored flowers are beautiful.

The warm sunlight is shining on us.

Everyone smiles.

Wa ha ha! Wa ha ha!

Everyone smiles.


Sisters, come here quickly.

Brothers, don't go away.

Hand in hand we will sing a song together.

How happy is our life!

Wa ha ha! Wa ha ha!

How happy is our life!

Chinese Text

我们的祖国是花园，

花园里花朵真鲜艳，

和暖的阳光照耀着我们，

每个人脸上都笑开颜。

娃哈哈！娃哈哈！

每个人脸上都笑开颜。


大姐姐你呀赶快来，

小弟弟你也莫躲开，

手拉着手呀唱起那歌儿，

我们的生活多愉快。

娃哈哈！娃哈哈！

我们的生活多愉快。
